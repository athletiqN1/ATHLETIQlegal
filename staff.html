Join a team's staff

A team invited you to its staff on ATHLETIQ. Staff accounts are free. The team's subscription covers you, and you never pay anything as staff.

How to join

  1. Get the ATHLETIQ app. During the beta the app is available by invitation: ask the team that invited you, or write to us.
  2. Open the app and tap "Invited to a team's staff? Join here" — the button at the top of the screen, not one of the role cards below it.
  3. Sign up with exactly the email address the invite was sent to.
  4. Verify your email address, then tap Join the team.

The invite expires 14 days after it was sent. If it has expired, ask the team to invite you again.

Tapped a role card by mistake?

Verify your email address and log in. Your invite appears before any payment screen: tap Join the team. If the account is new and you haven't used it yet, it becomes your staff account. Don't pay for a subscription to join a team's staff.

Already have an ATHLETIQ account?

An account you already use for another role (for example as an athlete, parent or scout) can't also be staff. Ask the team to invite a different email address.
